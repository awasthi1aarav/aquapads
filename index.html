About us & product

Designed around care, comfort, and responsibility.

AquaPads is an eco-friendly sanitary pad business concept aiming to address menstrual waste and textile waste together. The concept explores suitable processed textile fibres and water-responsive PVA-based structural components.

Our mission is to support menstrual privacy and dignity while considering sustainability from the product-design stage.
